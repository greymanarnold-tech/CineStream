Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your email address and we'll send you a link to reset your password.

Why is my video buffering?

Buffering can be caused by slow internet. Try lowering video quality in settings or check your internet connection speed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account Settings > Subscription > Cancel Subscription. Your access will continue until the end of your billing period.

Why was my payment declined?

This could be due to insufficient funds, expired card, or bank restrictions. Update your payment method in Account Settings.
